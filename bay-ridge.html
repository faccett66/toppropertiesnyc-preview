Bay Ridge

Waterfront walks, the Verrazzano, and Third Avenue’s restaurants.

Living here

Is Bay Ridge right for you?

Bay Ridge feels like a small town that happens to sit on the water. People walk the Shore Road promenade in the morning, grab coffee on Third Avenue, and know their neighbors by name.

If you love a morning walk by the water, you’ll love it here. You’ll find co-ops, classic rowhouses and some detached homes, plus the R train and a main street full of places to eat.

Search homes in Bay Ridge

Homes you’ll find

  • Pre-war co-ops along Shore Road and Fourth Avenue
  • Limestone and brick rowhouses on tree-lined side streets
  • Detached and semi-detached one- and two-family homes
  • Condos and smaller multi-family buildings
What you’ll love

The best parts of Bay Ridge

Shore Road Park

A long waterfront promenade along the Narrows, with views of the Verrazzano.

Third & Fifth Avenues

Two busy main streets full of restaurants, bakeries and everyday shops.

Getting around

The R train runs the length of the neighborhood, and a NYC Ferry stop sits at the 69th Street pier.

Owl’s Head Park

Rolling lawns and big trees at the north end of the neighborhood.
