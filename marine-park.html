Marine Park

Brick homes next to Brooklyn’s biggest park.

Living here

Is Marine Park right for you?

Marine Park is a true neighborhood. Families stay for generations, kids play ball in the park, and the brick homes on every block look after each other.

Families love Marine Park for the park itself — ball fields, trails and the salt marsh — and for blocks of solid brick homes where neighbors know each other.

The best parts of Marine Park

Marine Park

Brooklyn’s largest park, with ball fields, tennis courts, a running track and trails.

Salt Marsh Nature Center

Boardwalk trails through protected salt marsh along Gerritsen Creek.

Avenue U & Flatbush Avenue

Local shopping close by, with Kings Plaza just to the east.

Getting around

Served mainly by local and express buses, with easy access to the Belt Parkway.
