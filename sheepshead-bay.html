Sheepshead Bay

Boats on the bay, Emmons Avenue, and the B/Q train.

Living here

Is Sheepshead Bay right for you?

Sheepshead Bay is for people who want the water in their daily routine. Fishing boats head out from Emmons Avenue, and the beach is close by.

Want water views and an easy commute? You’ll find condos, co-ops and multi-family homes, fishing boats on the bay, and the B/Q train to Manhattan.

Search homes in Sheepshead Bay

Homes you’ll find

  • Condos and co-ops, many with water views
  • Multi-family homes
  • Detached and semi-detached houses
  • Bungalow courts on some of the older blocks
What you’ll love

The best parts of Sheepshead Bay

Emmons Avenue waterfront

Piers, fishing boats and restaurants along the bay.

Getting around

The B and Q trains stop at Sheepshead Bay station, with a straight ride to Manhattan.

Beaches nearby

Manhattan Beach and Plumb Beach are a short trip away.

Kingsborough Community College

A CUNY campus right on the water at the end of the peninsula.
